NORTH TERRACE DAILY TASK — V4.5.11
HOT FOOD PAPER-ENTRY WINDOW AND FIXED 23:00 CLOSING
Baseline: V4.5.10 full application

AGREED RULE
- Every day Hot Food administrative entry closes at 23:00 (shop local clock), independent of store closing.
- Hourly checks from each cooking session remain accessible for entry from staff paper logs until 23:00, including when their four-hour deadline has passed. No new automatic "Missed" status is applied.
- Scheduled hourly check time is retained in temperature reports. The actual reading time entered by staff and the actual Firebase submission timestamp remain available for audit.
- Four hours after Time Out Oven, any food remaining becomes EXPIRED and UNAVAILABLE FOR SALE. Keeping the form editable DOES NOT permit selling the food.
- A completed four-hour final check still uses one task per session and its entered final stock quantities; 0 = Sold Out, positive quantity = Waste with physical disposal confirmation.
- If required Hot Food records have not been completed by 23:00, unfinished check forms close, and the latest recorded quantity for each still-open food batch is booked as system-calculated end-of-day waste. If no later check is entered, the last system balance is used. Zero stock is Sold Out.
- Automatic end-of-day waste is clearly identified as system-calculated; disposalConfirmed remains false for positive quantities. The application NEVER claims staff physically disposed of food without their confirmation.
- A 23:00 closing audit record is created once for each remaining batch; repeated refresh/reconnect does not add waste twice.
- No browser can execute at an exact time if every device is closed/offline. The next connected opening reconciles the preceding day's 23:00 closing, with the scheduled cutoff recorded separately from when it was processed.
- Existing historical records and earlier completed final checks are preserved. Only new pending Hot Food tasks/batches are closed.
