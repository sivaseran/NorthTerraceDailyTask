NORTH TERRACE DAILY TASK — V4.5.13 CONSOLIDATED FULL RELEASE
Date: 21 October 2026
Base: V4.5.12 (which incorporates V4.5.10 session-based Hot Food and V4.5.11 23:00 cutoff)

THIS IS A FULL REPLACEMENT APPLICATION. Previous intermediate releases are not needed.

CHANGES SINCE V4.5.12
1. Navigation and branding
   - General View (index.html) is the landing page; its Login button opens the PIN page.
   - PIN Login shares the compact NT / NORTH TERRACE / Operations header, and has a Live View button returning to General View.
   - Manager Control now shares the same compact Operations header, displays Live status and Logout (no redundant General View button).
   - Manager Logout clears the authenticated session and goes directly to General View.
   - Browser Back to cached Manager page after logout is checked for a valid Manager session.
   - Standard Staff logout and installed Staff PWA logout also return to General View.
   - General View alarm bell and Live indicator remain.

2. Hot Food single-session four-hour final check
   - Time Out Oven is the cooking completion/display start time.
   - One 4-hour final task per *new* cooking session; includes every remaining product.
   - The editable Actual check time now defaults to Time Out Oven + 4 hours, NOT the time the popup was opened.
   - For items entered at zero remaining, Sell-out time becomes visible and defaults to Time Out Oven + 4 hours.
   - Sell-out time is editable between the oven-out time and 4-hour deadline, and cannot be after the actual check time.
   - Actual check time may be edited using a true paper-recorded time; actual entry timestamp is tracked separately.
   - Both fields are checked so a future time cannot be recorded as an event that has already occurred.
   - One combined confirmation summarises Sold Out vs Waste and confirms physical disposal when waste >0; no second disposal popup for NEW sessions.
   - The legacy per-product disposal code remains only as a safety fallback for existing legacy records, NOT generated for new sessions.
   - Improved readability in the session-level final check on phones and tablets.

CARRIED FORWARD UNCHANGED
   - Initial Cooking has No Cooking Required with a mandatory reason and audit entry. Additional Cooking does not.
   - Additional Cooking is cancellable and remains accessible all day through + Start Cooking.
   - Three hourly checks at oven-out +1,+2,+3 hours; one session final at +4 hours.
   - Earlier scheduled checks can be entered late from paper before 23:00; normal reports keep scheduled time and audit keeps entered time.
   - Daily Hot Food admin cutoff at 23:00, with automatic reconciliation/recovery and waste accounting.
   - All existing task assignment rules, Sunday rotation, Cleaning/Compliance, 90-day one-time limits and historical Firebase records.
   - Desktop-first Manager Schedule Editor, compact responsive PIN / General headers, General alarm settings.

VERIFICATION
