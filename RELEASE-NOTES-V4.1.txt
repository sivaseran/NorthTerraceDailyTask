North Terrace Daily Task — Consolidated V4.1
Built 09 October 2026 from the V4.0 live-derived V4.0.3 candidate

Primary screens:
- Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 (SM-X130), landscape: General View, PIN, task forms and audible alerts.
- Desktop: Manager, Schedule Editor, Effort Allocation, reports.

1. COMPLIANCE COMPLETION
- PIN authenticates staff only; Cancel never reports completion.
- All required compliance records and task completion commit together in one
  Firestore batch. A save error leaves the form open with its inputs intact.
- AM/PM temperatures require every reading and required recheck/management-aware
  confirmations. Empty values are not accepted as 0°C.
- SFBB Opening/Closing display complete configured checklists and a single
  Everything above is OK / Report an Issue confirmation. Old generic SFBB
  Closing placeholder is resolved through existing configuration fallback.

2. HOT FOOD
- Cooking, holding and expiry forms remain open on validation/save errors.
  Clear inline messages replace browser alert/prompt dialogs.
- Compact, touch-friendly panels for tablet landscape, sticky form actions.
- Missing core/holding readings rejected. Check times cannot be in the future;
  quantities and required FIFO sellout times validated.
- Sold-out batch times are editable inside the form; no native browser prompts.
- Temperature check form reveals waste reasons only when required and indicates
  mandatory waste for readings below the configured pass threshold.
- Manager report corrections also use editable in-app forms instead of prompts.
- Legacy fixed 'Check hot food temperature HH:MM' schedule entries are retired
  from future templates when the controlled module is enabled; future pending
  instances are cancelled, not deleted. Historical completed records remain.
- Controlled checks are generated by the Hot Food workflow as required.

3. DESKTOP TASK EDITING
- Effort Allocation has a working direct + Add Task panel with separate weekday
  time fields, name, effort, assignee and photo option.
- A new weekly task is created as one template with per-weekday future rules.
- Existing times are edited directly without first-keystroke resorting; the
  grid reorders after Enter or when the editor loses grid focus.
- Schedule Editor time field label is 'Time Order'.
- Confusing effort-only checkbox is removed from the visible filter toolbar.
- Stronger text contrast for selected/hovered/focused Schedule Editor rows.

4. SHOP ALARM AND CATEGORIES
- General View remembers alert preference on the shop tablet.
- Test Alarm button added for checking Galaxy Tab media volume/audio activation.
- Per-task 30 min and 5 min warnings; overdue alarm repeats approx. every 4 sec
  while General View stays active until Stop Alarm or View Tasks is pressed.
- Viewing a task stops the alarm and scrolls/focuses the relevant task but does
  not complete it; unfinished tasks alert again 10 min after acknowledgement.
- A newly overdue task can alert even during another task's acknowledgement.
- Report category labels: Daily Operations, Cleaning, Hot Food, Temperature Log,
  Daily Safety, Weekly Safety and SFBB.

5. HISTORICAL-DATA PROTECTION
- Completed operational task snapshots are not rewritten by reconciliation.
- Missing normal task instances are conditionally created in transactions so
  another browser's recent completion cannot be overwritten by a stale batch.
- Completion records are written to taskCompletionLedger. If a completed task
  instance is missing, planned generation can restore its completed state.
- Reconciliation skips unchanged snapshots, reducing Firestore writes.
- Existing cleaning rotation (from 08 Oct), toilet change (from 12 Oct), staff,
  PINs, scheduled effort, original assignees and historical completion are not
  otherwise modified by this release except the requested retirement of old
  fixed Hot Food temperature schedules.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS:
- Mock-browser workflow tests were completed; no production Firestore writes
  were performed during build and there was no access to the physical shop tablet.
- Browser audio cannot be guaranteed while Android locks/suspends the browser;
  keep the shop General View visible, screen awake, sound on, and test on-device.
- New completion-ledger collection must be writable under existing Firestore rules.
- Hot Food includes multi-document records; production save/retry/interruption
  behaviour requires a real-device/real-Firestore acceptance test.
