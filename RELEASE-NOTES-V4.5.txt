CRITICAL CORRECTION — EXACT FOUR-HOUR FINAL CHECK
* From the actual cooking-completion/display-start time, the ordinary hourly
  holding-temperature checks remain at +1, +2 and +3 hours (minimum 63°C).
* At +4 hours, the food becomes EXPIRED / NOT FOR SALE and a final temperature
  and actual physical shelf-quantity check becomes due. Automatic reconciliation
  does NOT waste the third-hour recorded balance.
* The staff member enters the ACTUAL remaining quantity on the shelf.
    - 0: SOLD OUT; waste remains 0; no disposal task. The actual sell-out
      time can be entered if known; otherwise it is recorded as "sold out
      between checks" and is not invented.
    - >0: the ENTERED final quantity is recorded as waste, exactly once, and
      the batch is closed to sale. The final display temperature is recorded.
      Staff then get a SEPARATE physical-disposal confirmation, with an
      optional Start Cooking another batch prompt.
* At the four-hour deadline the batch becomes unavailable for sale EVEN IF
  staff have not yet entered the final count. No waste is assumed until they
  do. Unfinished final checks stay pending/overdue.
* If no device was running, the next foreground sync recovers the overdue
  final check on the current date. It does NOT assume last recorded balance
  was wasted. The staff count remains mandatory.
* The final temperature check is included in the hot-holding reporting data.
  Completion and waste/disposal events are audited. Atomic transactions guard
  against two staff devices recording the same final check or waste twice.
* A stale 1st/2nd/3rd-hour form cannot be submitted after expiry.
* Same-product overlapping cooking batches remain independent.
* Existing legacy auto-expiry disposal records (if any) remain confirmable,
  but are NOT rewritten retroactively.

IMPORTANT PRODUCTION LIMITATIONS
* Offline tests cannot verify production Firestore rules or data, network
  latency, PWA cache refresh or the physical Galaxy Tab A11. Do an acceptance
  test using authorised live accounts before routine staff use.
* GitHub Pages cannot run scheduled background tasks when all browsers are
  closed; expired food must never be sold, and the overdue check will be
  surfaced when a browser opens again. Food safety procedures remain primary.
* An unusually high final count (greater than the last recorded balance) is
  rejected to avoid false stock/waste accounting; staff should correct the
  earlier stock error with a manager rather than entering an ungrounded count.
* Hourly holding checks that would cross midnight are not fully validated;
  do not treat this PWA as unattended overnight monitoring.
